Fixed a bug where registration crosshairs didn't align with card edges on non-poker card sizes. Crosshairs now sit exactly at the correct corners for all layouts and card sizes.

The sheet layout now automatically adjusts how many cards fit per page based on your selected card size. Smaller cards like Mini and Square will pack more onto a sheet instead of leaving empty space, and larger custom sizes will be handled gracefully too. Poker/Bridge/Euro sizes keep their familiar layouts as before.
